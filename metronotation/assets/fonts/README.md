[Cabin](https://github.com/impallari/Cabin) is used for the title and subtitle.
Its humanist letterforms follow the Johnston / Gill tradition of transit signage.
[Arimo](https://github.com/googlefonts/arimo) is used for move captions, case labels
and credits; its Arial-compatible advances preserve the drawing's spacing.

Both families are embedded directly into HTML with their copyright and license
notices. Offline opening and PDF export require no font download. Static fonts
preserve searchable text in the PDF; variable fonts are deliberately avoided.

The font files are not covered by the project's MIT software license. Font
installation on the reader's device is unnecessary.
